User story

As an inventory planner, I need to identify which SKU–warehouse combinations are exposed to stockout risk, understand why they are flagged, and prioritize a replenishment review—so I can investigate before stock coverage falls below supplier lead time.

Risk rule

P1 · Urgent

Out of stock, or days of supply is below supplier lead time.

P2 · Review

On-hand quantity is at or below the product reorder point, and the record is not already P1.

P3 · Monitor

No threshold is crossed. P3 items are shown by the model but omitted from the flagged action queue.

Decision ownership

The inventory planner reviews the evidence, checks open purchase orders and supplier constraints, then records or escalates the action in the organization’s approved workflow. This prototype does not update inventory or submit purchase orders.

What would make this production-ready?

  • Multiple dated snapshots and a governed refresh process.
  • Approved safety-stock and reorder policies.
  • Open PO, MOQ, pack-size and budget context.
  • Data owner, refresh timestamp, alert threshold and audit trail.

How this maps into Infor Birst

Translate the user story into three dashboard pages and one user-friendly subject area.

1

Display · Inventory Overview

KPI dashlets: on-hand units, estimated value, below-reorder records and stockout-risk records. Add warehouse/category charts and snapshot context.

2

Diagnose · Risk Drivers

Visualizer reports for warehouse/category risk, coverage vs lead time and SKU details. Add shared filters and drill-across where supported.

3

Decide · Review Queue

Prioritized detail report with decision basis and review-only quantity. Add a text note for assumptions, export and reconciliation.

Model & validation contract

Fact grain: one record per snapshot date × SKU × warehouse. Join the fact to Product on SKU and to Warehouse on Warehouse ID. Reconcile totals after joins; all current values should match the generated KPI CSV for the same snapshot/filter context.
