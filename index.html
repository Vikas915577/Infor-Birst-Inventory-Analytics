Business questions

  1. Which records have less coverage than supplier lead time?
  2. Which records are below reorder point?
  3. How does estimated inventory value vary by category and warehouse?
  4. Which rows should be prioritized for replenishment review?

KPI definitions

  • Days of Supply = On-hand ÷ Average Daily Demand
  • Stockout Risk = Days of Supply below lead time
  • Inventory Value = On-hand × Unit Cost
  • Below Reorder Point = On-hand at/below threshold

These are simplified demonstration rules, not production replenishment policy.
