# Data dictionary

All source data is generated synthetic demo data, not employer or client data.

- `dim_product.csv`: `sku_id`, product name, category, supplier, synthetic unit cost, reorder point, supplier lead time in days.
- `dim_warehouse.csv`: warehouse ID/name and region.
- `fact_inventory_snapshot.csv`: snapshot ID/date, SKU key, warehouse key, on-hand quantity and synthetic average daily demand.

Grain: one row per SKU × warehouse × snapshot date. Inventory value = quantity × synthetic unit cost. Days of supply = on-hand quantity ÷ average daily demand. When demand is zero/undefined, days of supply is unavailable and the demand source must be checked.

## Decision-support output fields (generated by analyze_inventory.py)

- `priority`: P1 urgent review, P2 replenishment review, or P3 monitor.
- `decision_basis`: specific rule causing the priority.
- `recommended_action`: suggested next step for a human reviewer. If stock is zero while demand is zero/undefined, the action explicitly asks the user to validate demand data before estimating replenishment.
- `illustrative_review_qty`: `ceil(daily demand × (lead time + 7 buffer days) − on hand)`, lower bounded at zero. The 7-day buffer is a demo assumption, not an order quantity or an approved policy.
